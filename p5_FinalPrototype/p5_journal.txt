Entry 1: 
I am currently brainstorming for the project. Even before getting the theme I had in mind an illustration of a bee in a field of grass flying over strange flowers. I thought that I could turn this wiew into a digital 3D space. With the theme of being stuck in a loop being given to us, it got me thinking of what I could do thematically in conjunction with the visuals of the bee and all. 

Here is what I have right now: You play as the bee and fly around protecting the flowers of a kid trying to grab them. You can attack the kid by stinging them and if you succeed the kid will cry and leave tears that will reach the ground and can generate new flowers. The kid will then try to reach those new flowers that just sprouted and you have to defend the flowers or else you die from lack of pollen. 

In order to keep it simple, the kid should move back and forth and reach flowers when they are close to them. I think it should move slow but steadily. When they are no flowers the kid goes back to running. 

Maybe if no flowers are being generated you actually have to sting the kid so that they cry. Maybe there could also be a percentage of the resistance of the kid to the stings. At first, there is a 100% chance for the kid to cry but as you sting them more and more the % lowers and they become more resistant to crying thus preventing the sprouting of flowers. 

I think the stinging of the kid should just be collision between the back of the bee and the kid. You can't go through the kid and if you hit the kid with the front of the bee nothing happens. The stinging happens if you hold a button while moving so that your bee flips its body so that the stinger is in front. 

I think the new flower being generated by the tear should appear at the position where the tear enters a colision with the ground. I think this would keep the system simple. 

There should also be invisible walls for the player to not leave the area where you play.

timer for collecting enough flowers
